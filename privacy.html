Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Glowra is a demo online shop created for a class project. This page explains what information the site uses.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • When you place an order: your name, phone number, delivery address, and the items you ordered.

How we use it

We use this information only to sign you in, save your orders, show your order history, and send you an order confirmation email. We do not sell or share your information for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and order emails are sent through Mailgun. No real payments are taken on this demo site.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To ask for your data to be deleted, email iamedaraprincess@gmail.com.
